--- layout: default title: Join Us description: How to reach out about research opportunities, senior theses, postdoctoral positions and collaborations with the Molecular Quantum Dynamics Lab. ---

The MolQuantDyn Lab is an active and growing group, and we are always happy to hear from motivated students interested in computational chemical physics, quantum dynamics and molecular spectroscopy.

Haverford undergraduates interested in a research project, a senior thesis, or a summer research position are encouraged to reach out directly by email with a short description of your background and interests. No prior programming or quantum mechanics experience is required — willingness to learn is what matters most.

Prospective postdocs, and external collaborators interested in our research are also welcome to get in touch.

See our Contact page to reach out.
