Conrad, Yijin and Heshu presented their Summer work at the KINSC Fall Symposium.

Conrad, Yijin and Heshu presented their Summer work at the KINSC Fall Symposium. Conrad worked on an MCTDH approach for HD+HD and HD+H reactive scattering, developing a method to simulate different types of reactive scattering with MCTDH. Heshu and Yijin built a code to simulate the collision of two molecules.
